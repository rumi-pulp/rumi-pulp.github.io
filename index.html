About

My name is Aiman, and I write.

I have always been drawn to Rumi, the poet. His Laila and Majnun have stayed with me, especially the idea that love can make a person lose himself only to discover something deeper.

Then there is Pulp, a completely different kind of love. Their songs speak of ordinary people, awkward desires, loneliness, and the strange beauty hidden inside everyday life.

Somewhere between Rumi's poetry and Pulp's modern melancholy, I found my own little identity.

So when people ask why I call myself Rumi-Pulp, the answer is simple:

Rumi gave me the poetry. Pulp gave me the soundtrack. I'm somewhere in between.

What I do now

Today I'm a Creative Writer, turning ideas into words that build brands and connect with people.
